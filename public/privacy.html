Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Sarah's BirdyDex is a free, hobby field guide for South East Queensland. You can use all of it without an account. This page says what it keeps about you, where, and how to remove it.

Without an account

Your sightings, outings, patches, photos and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us. Bird photos, sounds and maps are loaded from Wikimedia, OpenStreetMap and the Atlas of Living Australia, which see ordinary web requests from your device.

With an account

If you sign in (with an email link or with Google), we keep:

  • your email address, and your name from Google if you sign in with Google;
  • the display name and avatar species you choose, and whether to show you on the leaderboard;
  • a copy of your sightings, outings, patches and photos, including any places, coordinates and notes you added, and your app settings;
  • the groups you create or join.

We use this only to back up your data, keep it the same on your devices, and show the leaderboard. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use analytics or ad trackers.

Who can see what

  • Your sightings, places, coordinates, notes and photos are visible only to you. Photos are stored privately and opened with short-lived links.
  • The leaderboard shows other people only your display name, avatar species and counts (species seen, outings, badges). It never shows your sightings, places, notes or photos.
  • You can hide yourself from the Everyone leaderboard in Settings. Members of a group you join can see you on that group's board.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database, sign-in and photo storage (servers in Seoul, South Korea). Sign-in emails are sent through an email provider. Google sign-in is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we receive only your name, email address and profile ID.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then "Delete my account and cloud data". This permanently removes your account, your cloud copy of everything, and your place on leaderboards and in groups. Whatever is on your device stays there until you delete it in Settings or clear the browser's data.
