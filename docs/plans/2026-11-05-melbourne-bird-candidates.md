# Greater Melbourne: draft bird list

Built 2026-11-05 for [the Melbourne region design](2026-11-05-melbourne-region-design.md).

- **eBird:** all-time species lists and the last 30 days of sightings for the 37 councils (31 metro plus Greater Geelong, Surf Coast, Bass Coast, Macedon Ranges, Mitchell, Baw Baw).
- **ALA:** bird records since 2016 in lat −38.5…−37.4, lng 144.4…145.9 (the metro core; the build will use the full 37-council box).
- **Draft rule:** at least 3,000 ALA records, or (where ALA's name didn't match) on 25+ council lists and seen in 10+ this month. This mirrors SEQ, whose 181 birds were curated from roughly the commonest 190. Hand-curation comes next: drop the restricted-access-only waders nobody will tick, add a few prized ones (Hooded Plover is in; consider Orange-bellied Parrot, Powerful Owl, Pink Robin, Rufous Bristlebird).

**181 birds: 103 already in the app, 78 new.**
